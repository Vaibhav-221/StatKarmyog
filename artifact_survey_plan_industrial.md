# Sample Work Artifact: Survey Plan — Annual Survey of Industries (Mock)

_This is an original, Claude-authored mock document created for prototype demo purposes. It is not a real government document. Officer: OFF001 (Rakesh Kumar), Industrial Statistics Division._

## 1. Objective
To design the field survey plan for the current-cycle Annual Survey of Industries (ASI) coverage in the assigned district, covering registered factory units under the Factories Act.

## 2. Survey Design
The survey adopts a **stratified sampling** approach, with strata defined by industry classification (NIC codes) and factory employment size. Within each stratum, units are selected using a **systematic random sampling** procedure from the sampling frame maintained by the district industries office.

## 3. Sampling Frame and Coverage
The sampling frame consists of all registered factories as of the reference period. Coverage error is minimized by cross-verifying the frame against the latest business register update. Non-response units are flagged for a second-round revisit rather than substitution, to preserve the probability sampling structure.

## 4. Data Quality Checks
Before data entry, each schedule undergoes a two-stage validation: (a) range checks on employment and output figures against prior-year returns, and (b) consistency checks between raw material consumption and reported output value. Any unit failing both checks is flagged for supervisor review.

## 5. Data Collection Instrument
The survey schedule captures units of measurement, gross value of output, input costs, employment (male/female), and capital formation, aligned with the standard ASI schedule format.

## 6. Timeline
Field enumeration: 6 weeks. Data entry and validation: 2 weeks. Preliminary tabulation: 1 week.

## 7. Reporting
Aggregated results will feed into the Industrial Statistics Division's quarterly output for IIP-linked indicators.
